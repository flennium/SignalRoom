# SignalRoom brand asset brief

Use the prompt below with the designer. The words in the brief are constraints, not copy that must appear in the artwork.

## Designer prompt

Design a compact identity system for **SignalRoom**, a temporary live command room where small groups send notices, questions, decisions, and actions, then acknowledge what they have seen.

The brand should feel calm, precise, immediate, and useful under mild pressure. It should resemble a well-made field instrument or a clear public signal system more than a social network, gaming chat, or corporate collaboration suite. The interface uses a vertical signal rail, small status lights, restrained cool neutrals, dark ink, and selective color for meaning.

Create:

1. A primary wordmark reading “SignalRoom”.
2. A standalone symbol that works at 16 px, 32 px, and 128 px.
3. A horizontal lockup and a square app icon.
4. A one-color version for terminal documentation and stamps.
5. Light and dark variants.
6. A favicon set and social preview composition at 1200 × 630.

Explore a symbol built from one vertical rail and two or three signal points, with one point visibly active or acknowledged. Other valid directions are a room boundary interrupted by a transmitted pulse, or two aligned marks that suggest sending and receipt. Keep the geometry simple enough to redraw from memory.

Avoid speech bubbles, radio towers, Wi-Fi arcs, lightning bolts, gradients, mascots, shields, check marks inside circles, and generic connected-node diagrams. Do not use fake terminal text or tiny detail. The identity must not imply encrypted messaging or permanent record keeping.

Use the current product palette as a starting point, then refine it for contrast and reproduction. The main mark should work in near-black and white before color is added. Select one warm action color and one cool live-status color. Recommend exact HEX, RGB, CMYK, and accessible UI pairings.

Typography should be sturdy and human, with open counters and a clear capital S and R. Recommend an open-source display face and body/technical companion that can be self-hosted. Avoid overly geometric startup typography and science-fiction styling.

Present three distinct concepts. For each, include the idea in one sentence, construction logic, clear space, minimum size, color use, and examples on the web header, CLI README, favicon, GitHub social card, and Docker/GitHub release page. Show every concept at 16 px before presenting large mockups.

Deliver editable SVG source, outlined SVG, PDF, transparent PNG exports, favicon files, the social card, a short usage sheet, and license/source details for every typeface. Assets must remain legible without color and meet WCAG AA contrast when used as interface elements.

The final identity should communicate: **a temporary room, a clear signal, and visible receipt.**

## Selected direction

The product uses **Threshold**, the middle concept. The open boundary reads as a temporary room and the two orange points read as a signal crossing into it. It remains recognizable at favicon size and works in one color.

Current production assets:

- [standalone SVG mark](assets/signalroom-mark.svg)
- [horizontal SVG lockup](assets/signalroom-lockup.svg)
- [1200 × 630 social preview](assets/social-preview.png)

Core colors are Ink `#17212B`, Pulse `#F16632`, Canvas `#F3F6F8`, and white. Keep clear space around the mark equal to one orange point diameter. Do not place the mark below 16 px or add glow, gradients, shadows, or extra signal points.
